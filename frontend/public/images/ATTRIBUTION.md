## Generated images

The full photos for chicken 65, chicken dum biryani, gobi manchurian and onion uttapam were generated for this menu. `illustrative-dining-room.jpg` is an AI-generated dining-room scene used on the About page; it is not a photograph of Raaha. `raaha-reference-table.webp` is an AI-cleaned recreation based on the supplied visual reference, rather than a lossless extraction.

The images in `table-scenes/` are AI-generated, representative arrangements inspired by the supplied table reference and the representative food images; they are not photos taken at Raaha. Some scenes combine or adjust generated dishes to match a shared table arrangement. Food, dishes and shadows are flattened into each scene. Glasses or cups are used for drinks.

The transparent `dish-cutouts/` files used for category artwork are generated representative recreations in coordinated bowls, except sweet lassi, kulhad chai and adrak chai, which are background-removed derivatives of the corresponding Pexels photos listed above. The mini meal and vegetarian thali cutouts share one representative white-bowl image. The full preview/detail photos remain separate. `food-unavailable.svg` and `table-unavailable.svg` are original vector fallbacks.
